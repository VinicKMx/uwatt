# Code of Conduct

uWatt contributors are expected to keep discussion technical, respectful and useful.

## Expected Behavior

- Treat contributors with respect.
- Focus disagreement on evidence, design constraints and user impact.
- Be clear when measurements, hardware setups or assumptions are uncertain.
- Help maintain a project where new users can reproduce results safely.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Encouraging unsafe hardware operation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or restrict participation when behavior harms
the project or its contributors.
